Reminderski — window design

Both windows drawn from the photographs of the .NET original, at one to one. Monospace throughout, hairline borders, uppercase labels in grey, and exactly one filled black control per window for the action you actually want.

Palette

#0D0D0B ink #FFFFFF paper #8B897F label #0A0A08 bar
  • Borders are 1px, square, the same ink as the text.
  • Nothing is rounded, shadowed or gradient.

What this changes

  • The title bar is ours, not Windows'. Dragging, closing and the shadow become our job.
  • Buttons stop being system buttons and get drawn by hand.
  • The window no longer follows the Windows light or dark theme — it is always paper.

The character

Blinks, glances about, and stares at you once it has been ignored long enough. Use the switches under the notification to compare the full character against the plain face, and to watch each mood.

  • Frames are text on a fixed grid, so a mood costs a handful of glyphs to draw.
  • The timer only runs while a notification is open. Nothing animates otherwise.
  • The shortcut path never touches any of this, so it stays instant.

Open questions

  • Quick picks on the form: are 10 min / 1 hour / tomorrow 9am your real three?
  • Are the moods above the right set, and does the mood come from the snooze count, the waiting time, or both?
  • Window sizes are my guess: 620 × 470 and 520 × 260.
