What to judge it on

At a glance, in the corner of a screen. The notification arrives while you are doing something else. The silhouette has to read in half a second, at 152 px, out of the corner of your eye.

Does it earn a reaction? The point of a persona is that being looked at by one is harder to ignore than a rectangle. Switch to Ignored and see which of them you would least want to leave sitting there.

Has it got somewhere to go? Whatever we pick has to carry a dozen more moods later without redrawing the body: the eyes, the mouth and one pose are all that may change between frames.

The rules a frame obeys

  • Every frame in a set is the same rectangle of characters, so redrawing one never shifts anything around it.
  • Only the eyes, the mouth and one pose differ. The body is fixed. That is what makes a new mood cost a handful of glyphs instead of a new drawing.
  • Plain ASCII only. The tick had to come out of the Done button because Consolas has no glyph for it and Windows then measured the substitute at a width it did not draw it at.
  • The timer runs only while a notification is on screen. Nothing animates otherwise, so the idle cost stays where it is.

The moods today

  • Idle — first showing, nothing has happened yet. Blinks and glances about.
  • Waiting — pushed back at least once. Heavy lidded.
  • Ignored — 25 seconds on screen with no answer. Stares.
  • Done — not in the code. The window closes the instant you press Done, so nobody has ever seen it. Worth holding a beat for?

After the pick

  • Redraw the chosen one into src/persona.rs and set PERSONA_SIZE to the size in its caption.
  • Then the behaviour: what the mood is allowed to know. Today that is the snooze count and seconds on screen. FEATURES.md #21 would open the whole history to it.
  • Whether it turns up anywhere else — the tray icon, the form, the dashboard — or stays the notification's alone.
