Where I would land

Full size. It is Pinned at rest — same calm, same two features, nothing extra in the frame — and it has Pad's whole range waiting behind it. The brows only appear when the mood is not neutral, which means they read as an event rather than as decoration. At thirteen columns it also fills the box properly and gets the eyes far enough apart to turn, not just glance.

If thirteen columns is too much note, take Quiet or Still. They are the same idea at eleven columns; you lose the head-turn and keep everything else.

The one thing I would not keep is the jittering bottom edge. It is on Pinned too. It costs nine characters of movement every few seconds to say nothing, and it is most of why Pad reads as restless.

The quiet brow, in one line

  • The brow row is always there — the rectangle never changes size. It is simply blank in the neutral mood.
  • So the cost of brows at rest is nothing: same face as Pinned, same calm.
  • And the arrival of a brow is itself a signal. A face that has been level all evening and then draws its brows together is doing something a permanent brow cannot.
